# The life he's building

**First:** get paid as a product engineer for building products.

**Eventually:** a life like Tibo's (@tibo_maker, ~215k followers on X): build many products and
earn from them, because an audience is already there to sell to.

His own read of the gap: he can build; his weakness is **distribution**. Tibo can sell anything
he ships because people already follow him.

## Why the daily posting matters so much

The audience is the same thing in both stages. Posting daily about what he builds is the job
hunt now (recruiters and founders see a product engineer who ships), and it becomes the
distribution channel for his own products later. Every post counts toward both.

Tibo's marketing tips (from the post he pointed to,
https://x.com/tibo_maker/status/1917160487781122434, 2025-04-29) that apply here:
- Build the audience early, before you have something to sell.
- Go where your people already are.
- Show demos rather than explanations.
- Marketing is part of the work, not something done after it.
- If no single channel works, you don't have product-market fit yet.

## Already true

He's not starting from zero: Comunidad Antesis sells to real buyers in several countries, and
the Daisolar agent is a product for a paying business. He's already building products that earn
money, just at a small scale.

## Coaching notes

- Wanting less social media and wanting an audience don't conflict: *posting* builds the
  audience, *scrolling* is the time sink. Write posts here, publish them, then close the tab.
- One channel first: LinkedIn, because that's where recruiters are. Cross-posting to X is
  cheap; don't start a second strategy until LinkedIn is a daily habit.
- When he compares himself to Tibo and feels behind, remind him that Tibo's audience is years
  of daily posting. The only thing to compare is whether today's post went out.
