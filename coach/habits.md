# Habits

**One new habit at a time.** A habit becomes *active* only after the one before it holds a
10-day streak. Active habits are required; the rest are bonus: nice when they happen, never
counted as a miss. Each habit is a "when X, I will Y" plan, starts at under 5 minutes, and has
a relapse rule decided in advance.

**Weekends are off.** Streaks count weekdays only: Saturday and Sunday never break a streak or
trigger a relapse rule, and anything done on a weekend is a bonus.

## Active

### 1. Move (started 2026-10-22)

- **When** I finish breakfast, **I will** put on shoes and walk outside for 20 minutes, before
  opening the laptop.
- **Day-1 size:** shoes on, out the door, around the block. 5 minutes counts.
- **Number:** minutes moved, in the daily log.
- **Relapse rule:** if I skip 2 days in a row, the next morning is a 5-minute walk, no
  negotiation. Skipping isn't failing; skipping the restart is.
- **Graduates** after a 10-day streak: then grow the walk or add exercise, and activate
  habit 2.

## Queue (bonus until activated, in this order)

### 2. SelfControl block

- **When** I come back from the walk, **I will** start SelfControl (x.com, youtube.com,
  linkedin.com) before reading anything.
- **Relapse rule:** if I catch myself scrolling, start the block right then, even mid-day.

### 3. Daily LinkedIn post

- **When** the coach shows today's draft, **I will** tweak it and publish it before starting
  the SelfControl block.
- **Day-1 size:** publish the draft as is.
- **Relapse rule:** if I miss 2 days, the next post is 3 sentences about yesterday's work.

### 4. List one item for sale

- **When** I close the laptop at the end of the day, **I will** photograph one thing I don't use
  and send the photos to the coach, who writes the listing.
- **Day-1 size:** one photo of one thing.
- **Relapse rule:** if I miss 3 days, list the smallest, cheapest thing I can find.
