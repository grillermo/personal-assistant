# Weekly scoreboard

Goal: less social media, more job search. Fill one row every Sunday (or the first session of
the week) from the daily logs and `bin/social-time.sh 7`.

- **Social**: estimated hours on twitter/linkedin-feed/youtube in Chrome on this Mac (phone not
  counted; add his own estimate in Notes if he has one).
- **Job hunt**: hours on job-hunting work: LinkedIn posts, outreach, recruiter and networking
  conversations, applications he chose on purpose, interview prep, interviews.
- **Posts / Outreach**: LinkedIn posts published; personal messages to people (recruiters,
  hiring managers, former colleagues).
